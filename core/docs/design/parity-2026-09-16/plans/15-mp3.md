## 1. Summary and recommendation

**Vendor AOSP's PacketVideo `pvmp3` decoder (Apache-2.0), carried as a small C port, and
replace `dr_mp3` with it.** It is the only production-grade, fixed-point, MPEG-1/2/2.5 Layer III
decoder under an Apache-compatible licence that exists; everything else is float (dr_mp3/minimp3,
PDMP3), copyleft (libmad GPL, mpg123/ffmpeg-fixed LGPL, Symphonia MPL), RPSL (Helix) or a closed
binary (Espressif's `esp_audio_codec`).

Evidence gathered this session (details in §3 and §4):

| | pvmp3, ARMv4 build | dr_flac (shipping) | dr_mp3 (parked) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARMv4T instructions per second of audio, same 10 s of orchestral music, 48 kHz stereo | **15.7 M (128 kbps) / 18.9 M (320 kbps)** | 9.15 M (FLAC -5) | 219 M |
| ratio to dr_flac | 1.7× / 2.1× | 1× | 24× |
| accuracy vs ffmpeg's decoder | PSNR 103 dB, max 2 LSB | bit-exact | PSNR 120 dB |
| decoder state | 27.8 KB (one `pvmp3_decoderMemRequirements()` block) | ~37 KB arena high-water | ~96 KB arena high-water |
| code, `-Os`, ARM7TDMI | 24.3 KB text + 12.0 KB rodata | 52.6 KB text | 33.0 KB text |
| compiles freestanding, C11, `-Werror` with the firmware's flags | yes, after an 84-line patch (22 files) | yes | yes |
| runtime float | none (constants folded at compile time; no `__aeabi_f*` in the link) | none | soft-float everywhere |

pvmp3 costs about 1.7× the FLAC decoder's instructions on identical music. FLAC already streams on
the device, so the CPU question becomes "does 1.7× FLAC fit", and the answer is measured on the
device, not here: **the only thing this plan cannot do is prove real time on the host**. What it can
do (and did) is bound it: at a plausible ARM7TDMI CPI of 1.5–2.5 the 128 kbps decode needs 24–39 MHz
of the 80 MHz core, 320 kbps 28–47 MHz. Mitigations if the margin turns out thin are listed in §11
(IRAM placement of the hot loops, the parked COP). Do not spend time on option (3), porting
minimp3 to fixed point: it is a 4–8 week numerical project that ends with an unproven decoder,
and pvmp3 already passes the ISO full-accuracy class on real music.

Gapless is out of scope. ReplayGain for MP3 is out of scope (no gain applied; the FLAC path is
unchanged).

## 3. Performance evidence

### 3.1 Measured here: ARMv4T instruction counts under emulation

Method (all in `scratchpad/harness/`, reproducible): each decoder was cross-compiled with the
firmware's flags (`-std=gnu11 -mcpu=arm7tdmi -ffreestanding -fno-builtin -Os`, pvmp3 with
`-DPV_ARM_GCC_V4` so the `smull` inline ops and the four `.s` files are used), linked bare-metal
with the repo's `lib/mem.c` and libgcc, the clip `.incbin`'d, and run under **unicorn 2.1.4**
(QEMU-derived ARM CPU emulator, pip-installed into `scratchpad/venv`) with `emu_start(count=…)`
chunking, so the count is exact to ±250 k instructions. The decoded PCM was read back out of
emulated memory and compared, so each count is for a verified-correct decode.

Clip: 10 s of Beethoven Symphony No. 3 (Musopen recording, public domain; taken from micro-mp3's
`examples/decode_benchmark` at 128 and 320 kbps, 48 kHz stereo, cut with `ffmpeg -c copy`) and,
for FLAC, the same 10 s of ffmpeg-decoded PCM encoded with `flac -5` (587 KB). Also the repo's
1 s sine KAT vectors.

| binary | instructions | per second of audio | per MP3 frame (41.7/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| pvmp3 `-Os` V4, 128 kbps | 156.5 M | 15.65 M | 376 k |
| pvmp3 `-O2` V4, 128 kbps | 156.5 M | (identical — the hot code is asm/inline asm; keep `-Os`) | |
| pvmp3 `-Os` V4, 320 kbps | 189.3 M | 18.93 M | 454 k |
| pvmp3 `-Os` V4, KAT sine 44.1 kHz 128 kbps, 1 s | 14.75 M | 14.75 M | 360 k |
| dr_flac `-Os` (repo `flac.c`, CRC on), FLAC -5 of the same music | 91.5 M | 9.15 M | — |
| dr_flac, KAT sine 1 s | 3.5 M | 3.5 M | — |
| dr_mp3 `-Os` soft-float (repo `mp3.c`), 128 kbps | 2 190.8 M | 219.1 M | 5 257 k |

Reading: the pvmp3 cost is almost flat with content (sine vs orchestra 360 k vs 376 k/frame),
i.e. dominated by the fixed IMDCT + polyphase work, so these numbers are close to worst case for
128 kbps; 320 kbps adds 21 % in Huffman/requantisation. dr_mp3 needs 14× pvmp3, which is the
whole story of why it was parked.

### 3.2 Translating to MHz on the PP5022

The ARM7TDMI is a 3-stage, single-issue core; `smull` costs 1+m cycles (m ≤ 3), loads 3, taken
branches 3, and the PP5022 fetches from SDRAM through an 8 KB cache. A CPI of 1.5–2.5 is the
honest bracket for codec code with a 36 KB footprint:

| | CPI 1.5 | CPI 2.5 |
|---|---|---|
| pvmp3 128 kbps | 23.5 MHz | 39.1 MHz |
| pvmp3 320 kbps | 28.4 MHz | 47.3 MHz |
| dr_flac -5 | 13.7 MHz | 22.9 MHz |

Against the 80 MHz core (boosted during playback; `kernel/` scales to 30 MHz at idle — the codec
must be measured at the playback clock), 128 kbps MP3 leaves 40–55 MHz for the UI, the PIO disk
pump (the anti-skip buffer makes disk I/O bursty and, at 16 KB/s for MP3 versus ~110 KB/s for
FLAC, rarer) and the DMA feeder. That is the same shape of budget FLAC lives in today.

### 3.3 Published numbers (for calibration; none could be fetched from Rockbox)

- "Optimization of an MP3 decoder on the ARM processor" (researchgate 4058909, abstract via
  search): 128 kbps 44.1 kHz stereo in **27 MIPS on an ARM9TDMI** for an optimised fixed-point
  decoder. ARM9TDMI and ARM7TDMI share the v4T ISA; the ARM9's 5-stage pipeline and Harvard caches
  give it ~1.3–1.5× the IPC, so 27 ARM9 MIPS ≈ 35–40 ARM7 MHz — consistent with the bracket above.
- micro-mp3's ESP32 benchmark README (same decoder core, C fixed-point ops, Xtensa LX6 at
  240 MHz, `-O2`): 1.46 ms per frame at 64 kbps/48 kHz stereo = 6.1 % of a 240 MHz core ≈
  14.6 MHz-equivalent; the 320 kbps clip "runs roughly 55 % slower per frame".
- Rockbox's `CodecPerformanceComparison` wiki (libmad on PP5020/PP5022) is behind an Anubis
  challenge and the archive mirror is blocked from this host; the numbers there were **not**
  verified and are not used. The Rockbox tracker entry FS#5078 (search snippet) only shows that
  they measure codec cost in MHz on PP5022 and that moving functions to IRAM bought ~1.3 MHz.
- In this tree the only on-device decode figure is `player/player.c:448` ("~74 ms for a
  4096-frame step, i.e. ~18 ms per 1024 frames, 78 % of real time", added in `6baf8e7`); it was a
  UI-blocking wall-clock measurement (`PLAY_STEP_FRAMES` comment, `player.c:57`) that can include a
  synchronous `diskbuf` read, so it is an upper bound on FLAC's decode cost, not the DECODE %
  Boot Details would show. STATUS.md:424's "decode 61 ms/kframe" is from the spin-up stall
  incident, not steady state. **Take the FLAC DECODE % off Boot Details on the next device
  session before flashing MP3; the MP3 prediction is ≈1.7× that number.**

### 3.4 Accuracy

`scratchpad/harness/cmp.py` aligns by best offset and reports PSNR / peak error (ISO/IEC 11172-4
"full accuracy" is RMS < 2^-15/√12 ≈ 101 dB and peak ≤ 2 LSB at 16 bit):

| decode | reference | PSNR | max diff | RMS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pvmp3 host C-equivalent ops, 128 kbps | ffmpeg `mp3float` | 101.6 dB | 1 LSB | 0.273 LSB |
| pvmp3 host, 320 kbps | ffmpeg | 101.6 dB | 1 LSB | 0.272 LSB |
| **pvmp3 ARM `smull` asm path (emulated), 128 kbps** | ffmpeg | 103.4 dB | 2 LSB | 0.220 LSB |
| pvmp3 ARM asm path, 320 kbps | ffmpeg | 103.8 dB | 1 LSB | 0.213 LSB |
| pvmp3 ARM, repo KAT sine | committed `…_128k.mp3.ref.pcm` (dr_mp3's output) | 104.2 dB | 1 LSB | 0.201 LSB |
| dr_mp3 ARM soft-float | ffmpeg | 119.8 dB | 1 LSB | 0.033 LSB |
| dr_flac ARM | source PCM | bit-exact | 0 | 0 |

The asm and C-equivalent paths round differently (both within the standard), so the KAT
regression reference must be captured from the **same build variant** the test runs, and the
truth test must be a tolerance test (§8).

### 5.2 Wrapper (`codecs/pvmp3/mp3.c`) implementing `decoder.h`

Same external surface as today so `player.c` does not change: `mp3_decoder_ops()`,
`mp3_open_stream(decoder_t*, decoder_source_t*, const decoder_alloc_t*)`, memory `open` for the
KAT. State (one arena allocation):

```
typedef struct {
    uint8_t  *pv_mem;            /* pvmp3_decoderMemRequirements() = 27.8 KB (21.7 KB with BUFSIZE 2048) */
    tPVMP3DecoderExternal ext;
    decoder_source_t *src;
    uint8_t   frame[2048];       /* one compressed frame, max 1441 B + resync slack            */
    int16_t   pcm[1152 * 2];     /* one decoded frame, 4.6 KB; read cursor for partial decode() */
    int       pcm_len, pcm_pos;  /* frames staged / consumed                                     */
    mp3_stream_info_t info;      /* audio_start, audio_end, first header, xing (5.3)             */
    uint64_t  next_frame;        /* PCM position of the next decoded frame (seek/clock)          */
} mp3_state_t;
```

- `open`: `mp3_stream_scan()` (5.3) through the read-ahead source; refuse sample rates the HAL
  cannot clock (16 000, 12 000, 11 025, 8 000 → `DECODER_ERR_UNSUPPORTED`; MPEG-2 22.05/24 kHz
  podcasts are fine), refuse free-format bitrate; `pvmp3_InitDecoder`; fill
  `sample_rate/channels/bits_per_sample=16/total_frames`.
- `decode(out, max_frames)`: drain the staged frame; when empty read 4 header bytes, validate,
  compute the frame length, read the rest into `frame[]`, hand exactly **one** frame to
  `pvmp3_framedecoder` (AOSP `main`'s `validate_input()` walks the whole buffer it is given
  every call, so one-frame-per-call is also what keeps it O(1)); on `SYNCH_LOST_ERROR` /
  header failure advance one byte and resync (bounded: give up after 64 KB → EOS); on other
  decode errors count it, emit the frame pvmp3 produced (it mutes) and continue. Mono comes out as
  one sample per frame; the player up-mixes. Layer I/II frames return `UNSUPPORTED_LAYER` → EOS.
- `seek(target_frame)` (5.5), `close`: arena free thunks (no-ops), `d->ops = NULL`.
- `crcEnabled = 0`, `equalizerType = flat` (bass/treble stay in the WM8758).

### 5.3 Stream scan: tags, Xing/Info, VBRI, duration (`mp3_frame.c`)

Pure functions over bytes, one streaming driver:
1. **ID3v2 prefix**: `"ID3"`, version 2.2/2.3/2.4, flags; size sync-safe; footer flag adds 10.
   Loop (chained tags happen). Everything else in the tag is skipped by size — the reader in 5.4
   parses frames from the same bytes but is separate.
2. **Tail**: `SEEK_END` (the sources honour it; `readahead.c:341-349`, `diskbuf.c:275-287`):
   `"TAG"` at −128 → ID3v1 (genre byte, track in v1.1 — only used to bound the audio region);
   `"APETAGEX"` at −32 (or −160 with ID3v1) → APE tail by its header size. `audio_end` is the
   file end minus these. (Does the same job dr_mp3 did at init, `dr_mp3/mp3.c:194-199`.)
3. **First frame**: from `audio_start` find a header whose computed length lands on another valid
   header with the same version/layer/rate (two-header validation; the existing dr_mp3 KAT vector
   and the LAME-encoded clips have no junk, but user files do).
4. **Xing/Info**: at first frame + side-info size (MPEG-1: 32 B stereo / 17 mono; MPEG-2/2.5:
   17 / 9). `"Xing"` (VBR) or `"Info"` (CBR) + flags: frames (u32), bytes (u32), TOC (100 × u8,
   percent-of-file → percent-of-bytes), quality. If `"LAME"`/`"Lavc"` follows: encoder delay and
   padding (two 12-bit fields at +141 from the tag start) — used only to correct
   `total_frames` so the clock and the progress bar match ffprobe (which subtracts them).
   That frame yields 1152 (576) samples of silence from pvmp3; skip it (do not decode it).
5. **VBRI** (Fraunhofer; 32 bytes after the header): frames + bytes; TOC optional — support the
   count, ignore the TOC in v1.
6. **`total_frames`**: Xing/VBRI frames × spf (1152 MPEG-1, 576 MPEG-2/2.5) − delay − padding;
   else CBR estimate `(audio_end − audio_start) × rate / (bitrate/8)` (exact for CBR, the
   dr_mp3 wrapper's rule at `dr_mp3/mp3.c:267-280`); 0 only if the first header is unreadable.
   The player copes with 0 (`player.c:1066-1071`) and clamps seeks by size (`:2114-2124`).

### 5.4 On-device metadata (`id3_meta.c`)

`track_open` (`player.c:1033-1036`) and `player_probe_meta` (`:1851`) call `flac_meta_read`; on
an MP3 it returns `have = 0`, so Now Playing falls back to the filename (`main.c:3495`) and the
no-index scan writes duration 0. Add `int id3_meta_read(decoder_source_t*, flac_meta_t*)`:
a streaming, bounded ID3v2.3/2.4 reader (TIT2/TPE1/TPE2/TALB/TCON/TRCK/TDRC|TYER; encodings 0/1/2/3
with real UTF-16 → UTF-8 conversion, not the '?' downconvert of `tag_mp3.c:174-195`, since the
atlas and `copy_display_name` are UTF-8 now; TCON numeric → the existing table; skips APIC by size;
tolerates unsynchronisation by treating it as opaque; caps the tag walk at 1 MB) plus

one-line edits in `player.c`, none in `main.c`. The `flac_meta_t` name stays (renaming it is a

### 5.5 Seek

Target PCM frame → byte offset: Xing TOC when present (100-point table, linear interpolation
between entries, mapped over `[audio_start, audio_end)`), else proportional by `bytes ×
target/total_frames`. Then: back up one frame's worth (the bit reservoir can reference up to 511
bytes of earlier main data), resync to a validated header, `pvmp3_resetDecoder`, decode and
discard that one warm-up frame (pvmp3 mutes an under-filled reservoir rather than failing), and
set `next_frame` to the estimated PCM position of the landing frame. Accuracy is one frame
(±26 ms at 44.1 kHz) plus the TOC's granularity (1 % of the file, ≤ 3 s on a 5-min VBR track,
exact for CBR) — the same class as dr_flac's binary search landing on a frame. Cost: one
`diskbuf` window reset + one read-ahead refill + one frame decode, so **resume can seek MP3 now**:
drop the `fmt == 0 &&` gate at `main.c:4682` and rewrite the comment at `:4609-4612`. The
`player.c:2114` size clamp stays as the last line of defence for `total_frames == 0`.

## 11. Risks

- **Real time on the device is unproven** (only bounded). If DECODE % comes back > 80 %:
  (a) move `pvmp3_polyphase_filter_window`, `pvmp3_mdct_18`, `pvmp3_dct_16/9` and the Huffman
  tables to IRAM (there is a 96 KB region, `boot/linker.ld`; a `.iram.text` section + crt0 copy
  is ~a day; Rockbox reports ~1.3 MHz per hot function on this SoC); (b) `-O2` for the wrapper's
  bit-reader path only (measured: no gain for the decoder proper); (c) the parked COP as a decode
  core is the structural escalation and a separate plan. Do not ship 8 kHz MPEG-2.5 to dodge
  the question; it is refused at open.
- **`validate_input()` in AOSP `main`** re-walks the given buffer per call: harmless with
  one-frame-per-call, catastrophic if someone later hands it the read-ahead window. Assert on
  `inputBufferCurrentLength == frame_len` in the wrapper.
- **Asm vs C rounding differ** (103 dB vs 101.6 dB): the exact-match KAT reference is captured
  from the host (C-equivalent) build; the ARM build is only held to the tolerance test. Say so in
  the vectors README so nobody "fixes" a 1-LSB difference.
- **Junk-tolerant sync** is where MP3 decoders crash: the wrapper's resync is bounded and every
  read is length-checked; add the fuzz-style `mp3-robust` cases before the device sees a user
  library.
- **Index parity**: Go and Python must agree on MP3 duration (Xing − delay − padding, truncated)
  or the byte-identical-index guarantee breaks; the parity test is the gate.
- **UTF-16 tags** in ID3 are the norm for Windows-tagged files; the old '?' downconvert would
  put garbage in the marquee — the new reader must convert properly (tested).
- **Chained/oversized ID3v2** (embedded 1 MB art): the scan walks it through the 32 KB
  read-ahead, one refill per 32 KB; fine, but cap the walk.
- **`decode_us_per_kframe` divisor** assumes 44.1 kHz; at 48 kHz the percentage under-reports by
  8 %. Fix with the feature.
- Patent disclaimer is vendored and mentioned; not a legal review.
